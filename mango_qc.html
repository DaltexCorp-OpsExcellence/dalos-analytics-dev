KPI Definitions — Quality Control (Mango)
How each metric on this dashboard is calculated
Container-level basis. All defect rates are averaged at container level: samples are averaged within each container first, then those container figures are averaged together. Mango defects have no major/minor split — they are grouped into 5 type-sections (External, Insect, Size Variation, Rot, Harvest).
