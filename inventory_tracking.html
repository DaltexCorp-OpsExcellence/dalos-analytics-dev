ABC Analysis
ABC classes (Pareto 80/20)

A — vital few: ~10% of items that hold ~80% of your on-hand value. Watch these closely.

B: the middle — the next ~15% of value.

C — long tail: most of your items, but only ~5% of value. Don't over-manage these.

Computed per category: sort items high→low by value; A up to 80% of the category's value, B up to 95%, C the rest.

Pareto classification by on-hand value within each category — A = items making up the first ≤80% of category value (the vital few), B = the next ≤95%, C = the long tail. Overstock = on-hand above a target of (max annual usage ÷ 365 × target days).

Reorder & Safety Stock
Statistical reorder points from the transaction ledger — monthly OUTBOUND per item gives mean demand D̄ and std-dev σD; lead time L is estimated from the average interval between INBOUND receipts. Safety Stock = Z·σD·√L · Reorder Point = D̄·L + Safety Stock (L in months). An item needs reordering when on-hand ≤ its reorder point.
What the formulas mean

Reorder Point = D̄·L + Safety Stock — the "time to reorder" line: the stock you'll use up while waiting for delivery, plus a cushion. When on-hand drops to it, reorder.

Safety Stock = Z·σ·√L — the just-in-case cushion. Bigger when demand is bouncy (σ), the wait is long (L), or you want to play it safe (Z).

Candy-stand version: reorder point = "call the truck now" line; safety stock = the backup pile so you never run out on a surprise-busy day.
